How we check a deal

  1. Read the price at the store itself, or in a deals roundup that names the store.
  2. Check the maths: the discount shown is always (usual − sale) ÷ usual.
  3. Give an honest verdict: NEW LOW means the lowest price we could find evidence for; STRONG DEAL is a big cut on a rarely-discounted product; ROUTINE SALE is the usual seasonal cut; INFLATED means the “usual” price looks pumped up.
  4. Note the catch: where a store shows a “comparable value” instead of a past selling price, we say so — judge those by the sale price alone.

Amazon raised the list prices of some Echo, Fire TV and Kindle devices earlier this year, so their discounts are measured against a higher starting price. That is noted on those cards.
